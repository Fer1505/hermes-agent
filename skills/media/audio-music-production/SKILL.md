# Audio & Music Production

Use this umbrella for audio/music tasks spanning creative writing, AI music prompts, generated audio, sound effects, and audio feature visualization.

## Songwriting and AI music prompts

Use when the user wants lyrics, parody, a Suno/music-generation prompt, structure help, genre/style direction, or song adaptation. Craft comes first: choose emotional arc, structure, rhyme/meter, sonic palette, and performance notes before prompting a generator.

## Audio generation with AudioCraft

Use for MusicGen text-to-music, AudioGen sound effects, melody-conditioned generation, stereo generation, and EnCodec workflows. Confirm install/GPU constraints, model size, duration, prompt, and output format. Verify produced files exist and can be opened/played before reporting completion.

## Audio analysis and spectrograms

Use `songsee` or equivalent tools for spectrograms, mel/chroma/MFCC panels, time slices, tempo/loudness/flux, and visual evidence from audio. Keep the generated image path and analysis settings in the final answer.
